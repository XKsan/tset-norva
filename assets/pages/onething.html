章鱼实时编辑RNA

Octopuses Edit Their Own RNA

揭示与大多数动物不同，章鱼在DNA转录后会大量编辑其RNA分子。这使它们能够根据环境实时微调蛋白质的产生。

Unlike most animals, octopuses extensively edit their RNA molecules after they've been transcribed from DNA. This allows them to finely tune protein production in response to their environment, on the fly.

含义这种独特的生物机制提供了惊人的适应性，暗示了复杂智慧在僵硬基因代码之外的并行演化路径。

This unique biological mechanism offers incredible adaptability, hinting at a parallel evolutionary path for complex intelligence beyond rigid genetic code.
